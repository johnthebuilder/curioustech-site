Sec. 02 · Process

How we work

Software has overpromised to shops for thirty years. So we don’t ask you to take anything on faith — every engagement starts with a pilot on your own files.

  1. Step 01

    We start with the work

    We sit down with the people doing the job and watch where the hours actually go. Nothing gets built until the problem is specific.

  2. Step 02

    You pilot it on your files

    Before you commit, the tool runs on your own job files. You see it working in your shop — not a sales deck.

  3. Step 03

    License it, keep the support

    Simple licensing. Support comes from the engineer who built the tool, and it keeps getting fixed after it ships.
