    Limits, stated plainly: this viewer draws plate-style parts — ST header, AK outlines and IK cutouts (corner-radius values are read but corners are drawn as straight segments), BO holes and slots, SI marks as crosses with text. PU/KO marking geometry and unknown blocks are counted, not drawn. Parts with multiple faces (beams: o/u/h) draw overlaid flat, not folded. Header decoding follows the standard field order; detailer dialects may shift fields. View-only — no editing, no export.
