    Limits · stated plainly

    What this viewer reads: ASCII DXF only — binary DXF and DWG files are detected and refused with an explanation, not a spinner. What it draws: LINE, CIRCLE, ARC, LWPOLYLINE (bulge arcs approximated with short segments), POLYLINE/VERTEX, and TEXT. Everything else — SPLINE, INSERT, DIMENSION, hatches — is counted and listed in the File panel, not drawn, so you know exactly what you’re not seeing. Colors are per-layer for legibility, not your CAD colors. View-only: no editing, no export. Coordinates are shown as-is in the file’s own units. Format details in the DXF guide →
